Introduction

I’m a recent Cybersecurity graduate from the University of Maryland Global Campus with a strong foundation in technical troubleshooting and customer support. My interest in IT began in high school, where I developed practical skills repairing and maintaining computers. Since then, I’ve applied that knowledge to assist individuals in my community with a wide range of technical issues. I’ve strengthened my technical abilities by earning the CompTIA A+ certification and continuing to pursue additional IT credentials to prepare for a role in Help Desk or Technical Support.

My Tech Background

I have several years of hands-on experience building, maintaining, and troubleshooting PCs, including hardware upgrades, diagnostics, and system optimization. I support friends, family, and local community members through my work as an IT volunteer at St. Joseph’s. I also maintain my home network, including router and modem configurations, and operate a dedicated lab system where I practice common Help Desk scenarios and expand my technical skill set.

My Motivation

I enjoy the problem-solving aspect of IT and the opportunity to help people get their technology working the way it should. Turning confusion into clarity for someone is something I find incredibly rewarding. My long-term goal is to build a career in cybersecurity, and I see Help Desk as the ideal starting point to continue developing my technical expertise and customer service skills.
